## Vision
An internal and external local-marketing media network on shop TVs. The shop's own content (reel menus, offers, social proof, live counters) plays on its screen; nearby advertisers rent the remaining slots through a self-serve, largely autonomous ad flow with QR recall. Starting in Bengaluru, restaurants and cafes first.

## Problem
Small local businesses are priced out of city-wide out-of-home media and get little from geo-fenced social ads, which miss people who are already on the street, near the shop, and ready to buy.

## Solution
Turn internet-connected TVs in local shops (Display Units) into a hyperlocal network:
- **For the shop (ZupPartner):** free, trendy, Instagram-like content on its own TV, built from its own reels; partner payout from ad revenue; control over which ads play.
- **For the advertiser:** cheap hyperlocal slots within walking distance, self-serve, measured by QR scans and coupon redemptions.
- **Partnering, not rebuilding:** shop-signage software (e.g. Gloo) is a candidate partner. **[assumption]** Gloo's current feature set is unverified.

## Why it might work, and what is unproven
- Internal media is the hook that gets screens on walls; external ads are the revenue. **[assumption]** Shops will adopt for the content alone.
- Advertising to people already near the shop. **[assumption]** Neighbourhood advertisers will pay for shop-TV slots; no price or fill rate is tested.
- AI core (ad generation, geo-temporal optimisation, foot-traffic-lift estimation). **[assumption]** Not built; foot-traffic lift is hard to prove, and in-store TV networks have often struggled for that reason.

## Market
Earlier estimate of the Bengaluru local-advertising market (about 500,000 SMEs at about 6,000 per year each, roughly 300 crore TAM, 75 crore SAM). **[assumption]** Unsourced; to be redone with real data.

## Competitors to study
Self-serve digital out-of-home networks already operate in India (for example AdOnMo, Pixelspot, The Hype Loop), plus closed-signage and traditional out-of-home players. The possible gap is very small neighbourhood shops plus QR recall.
