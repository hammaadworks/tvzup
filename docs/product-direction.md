# tvzup: product direction (2026-11-26)

Status: idea stage. No shopkeeper or advertiser has been interviewed yet. Everything below is a hypothesis to test.

## One-line pitch
An internal and external local-marketing media network on shop TVs: the shop's own content plays on its screen, and nearby advertisers rent the remaining slots.

## Product, as a combination of parts
| Part | Role | Reference / note |
|---|---|---|
| Internal media | The hook: sells to the shop owner | Own offers, menu, social proof on the shop's own TV |
| Scrollable menus (Yum Yum) | Internal media for restaurants and cafes | Instagram-like, trendy. Owners' own food reels (connected Instagram or direct upload) become the TV menu |
| Live counter and celebration | Internal media | Smiirl-style follower counter in software, with jingle/animation on new follows |
| Ad engine + QR | The revenue: external local marketing | Self-serve upload, locality and budget, autonomous (AI) ad-agency flow; QR lets viewers re-watch and save the ad |
| Screen player | Supply side | Own player; optionally debloat Android/Google TV with Clean Your TV (open source) |
| Partner layer | Build vs partner | Gloo (shop signage SaaS) is a candidate partner rather than a rebuild. Unverified: whether Gloo already has reel menus or an ad marketplace; check before any pitch |

## Strategy
- Internal media gets screens onto walls (low-friction offer to the owner). External ads monetise the free slots between internal content.
- Do not rebuild what a partner already does well; build only what is missing (ad demand, reel pipeline, trendy menu design).
- Start with restaurants and cafes (menu use case); pharmacies and groceries follow for ads.

## Open questions
- Can reels be pulled from an owner's connected Instagram (Graph API, business accounts), or must owners upload them?
- What does Gloo offer today, and would they partner?
- Will a neighbourhood advertiser pay for shop-TV slots? What rate and fill are realistic?
- Ad-content and income-model ethics: confirm with a qualified scholar; keep a platform-level allowlist of ad categories.

## Smallest next test
Pick one cafe with food reels on Instagram, show its own reels on a TV mockup, and see whether the owner wants it. Then sell one ad slot by hand to a nearby business.
